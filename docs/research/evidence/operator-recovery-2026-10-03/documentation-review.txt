## Findings

Two Minor qualifications were found. No Important or Critical factual or clarity error was found. The coordinator accepted both findings.

1. **Operator replay availability needs its epoch condition.** docs/operator-recovery.md:77 and102 promise exact replay without stating the replay-floor limit. Runtime and StorageState check the operator epoch before receipt lookup. A retained operator receipt can remain as audit evidence after its request returns `IdentityExpired`. Current authority also controls disclosure. Qualify replay by current authority and retry epoch checks, and distinguish retained evidence from replay eligibility.
2. **The CLI epoch mechanism needs its command scope.** docs/retention.md:41 says CLI mutations accept `--retry-epoch N`. Resource mutation commands accept that option. `work retry` and `work reconcile` instead require `retry_epoch` inside their exact request file. State both mechanisms without permitting an epoch change on retry.
